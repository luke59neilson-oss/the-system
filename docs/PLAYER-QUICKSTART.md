# Player quickstart

One page for the table. You need a phone or laptop on the *same wifi* as the
GM's laptop; nothing to install.

## Join

1. Scan the QR code the GM shows, or type the address exactly as shown
   (`http://`, numbers and the `:port`), for example `http://192.168.1.20:30333`.
2. Under **Choose your character**, tap your character. Next time the page
   offers **Welcome back · Continue as …**.
3. The badge at the top says **Connected**. If it says **Reconnecting…**, wait:
   it catches up on its own. Can't connect at all? Tell the GM (see
   `docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md`).

**Switch character** (top bar) goes back to the list. **Sound: off** turns the "DING!" chime on
(and back off), with a volume slider.

## Your status screen

- **Status**: name, race, class, profession (from E-grade), HP / MP /
  Stamina, the nine stats in tenths (23 → 2.3), titles and skills. Values
  update live as the GM plays.
- **Free points · [Assign]**: each level-up's free points. Use **−** / **+**,
  then **Review** and **Confirm**. A batch locks once confirmed; ask the GM if
  you need a change.
- **Skill selection · [Choose]**: when the System offers new skills. New
  options are listed first, passed-over ones below. Pick one, then
  **Confirm** (or **Back**). It can wait for a break in play.
- **[Evolve]**: appears when the GM releases your evolution. Look at each
  path and its stat bonus, **Choose** one, then **Evolve**.
- Tap any skill (shown as `[Name (Rarity)]`) to read its description, effects and mastery
  marks, and to give it a **Nickname**.
- **Professions**: from E-grade the GM offers professions that fit what you
  have done. Pick one or wait; once you choose, the list locks.

## The other panels

- **Party**: everyone's race, class, levels and pool bars.
- **Inventory**: your currency, **Equipment** (**[Equip]** / **[Unequip]**),
  **Items** (**[Give]**), **Give** currency and **Propose a trade** to another
  player or the GM. Offers to you show under **Trades** with **[Accept]** /
  **[Decline]**. Nobody sees what you hold through a trade.
- **Quests**: the current quests, days remaining and, if shown, World
  Stability.
- **Practice**: **Log practice** when you use a skill or stat in a way that
  mattered; it feeds the GM's mastery marks and skill offers. During a
  montage, write **Your one-line focus** and **Send focus**.
- **Identify**: pick something you know of to read what the System tells
  you. Things too far above you show `[??? – lvl ???]`.
- **Notes**: **New note** in **My journal** (private; tick **Let the GM read
  this entry** to share it) or **Party notes** (the whole party and the GM).
  Markdown and tags work; search finds any note.
- **Notifications**: every "DING!" and change, filtered by **Show** (Level-ups
  and upgrades, Everything, Class, …) and searchable. **System messages** are
  the GM's broadcasts. **Show older** loads the rest.

What you see is yours alone: other players never see your skills' effects,
your journal or your inventory.
