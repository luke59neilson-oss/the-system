- D35 · 2026-10-21 · Class spread is split cumulatively by a highest-deficit rule: each point goes to the stat furthest below its weighted share of all spread received so far (ties to stat order). This deliberately departs from ROADMAP's "largest remainder" for spread (XP shares still use largest remainder): a per-level largest-remainder split rounds the same way every level, so with 4 points over 9 equal stats Strength–Vitality would get every point. Totals sum exactly per level and every stat stays within about one point of its share (worst seen in review: 1.04); the rule can differ from largest remainder on a single level (pinned in tests). Characters store integer class weights (0–1000, equal when omitted) and spread totals; reshaping the weights (M1.10) resets the totals. · Fair long-run spread without per-level rounding bias.
- D36 · 2026-10-21 · XP shares (largest remainder, ties to the earlier participant) sum to the award; the above-level bonus is extra XP on top of a share, measured against the character's race level and derived when the event applies. The award event stores the shares and enemy level, not the level-ups. · DESIGN §5 makes the GM's split the input; level-ups follow from the rules.

- D38 · 2026-10-21 · Free-point batches are addressed by race level (race level never falls, so it is unique). Players assign their own batches (`freePoints.assign`, the first player command); a batch must be assigned in full and then locks. Only the GM overrides (`freePoints.override`): a new full allocation stays locked, `null` unassigns and unlocks. Overrides that push a stat below 0, and unassigning a batch that is not assigned, are rejected; pools re-clamp. An override is the GM's final say: undoing an earlier player assignment leaves a later override in force. Player authority is checked on every event entering the log (append and replay), not only in `execute`. Accounting is by delta: after a GM `setStats`, an override subtracts the batch's recorded allocation from whatever the stat now is, so if a GM edit took a stat below that batch's points, the batch can't be overridden or unassigned until that edit is undone. · DESIGN §4 "assigned once, then locked; the GM can override", with every change an undoable event.
